Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common tax, legal, and compliance questions.

What is the turnover limit for GST registration?
For services, the limit is ₹20 Lakhs (₹10 Lakhs in special category states). For goods, the limit is ₹40 Lakhs (₹20 Lakhs in special category states). However, mandatory registration is required for inter-state supply and certain other conditions.
When is the due date for ITR filing?
For individuals and non-audit cases, it is typically July 31st of the assessment year. For audit cases and companies, it is usually October 31st.
What documents are required for a rent agreement?
You need ID proofs (Aadhaar/PAN) of the landlord and tenant, ownership proof (like electricity bill), and passport-size photographs.
How is stamp duty calculated for a sale deed?
Stamp duty is a state subject and usually ranges from 4% to 7% of the property value or circle rate, whichever is higher.
What should I do if I receive a Show Cause Notice from the GST department?
Do not ignore it. Contact our experts immediately. We will help you draft a proper legal reply with facts and supporting evidence to represent your case before the authorities.
